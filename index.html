Made for people who sell on WhatsApp and Instagram

You already have the customers. Zémi handles the part between your door and theirs, so you can keep selling instead of crossing town.

  • Pickup within 15 minutes

    A rider from the nearest stand comes to your door. No trip to the market, no waiting at a junction.

  • Follow the ride on WhatsApp

    You and your customer get a live tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves. There is no app to install.

  • Cash on delivery, paid out tonight

    The rider collects the payment from your customer. The money reaches your MTN or Moov Mobile Money account by 8 p.m.

  • Covered up to 50 000 FCFA

    If a parcel is lost or damaged on the way, we refund its declared value within 48 hours.

Three messages from order to payout

  1. 1

    Send us the order

    Message the pickup address, your customer's number and the amount to collect.

  2. 2

    The rider picks it up

    The rider confirms the price, takes a photo of the parcel and shares the tracking link.

  3. 3

    Your customer pays, you get paid

    The customer pays the rider on delivery. You receive the amount minus the delivery fee the same evening.

Prices from Ganhi

One price per route, decided before the rider leaves. No fuel surcharge, no rush-hour fee. Medium parcels add 300 FCFA.
